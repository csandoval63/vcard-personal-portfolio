    Questions

    How does event pickup work?

    Order online, choose the event, and show your order number at the booth. Your cans are packed and waiting, so you skip the line.

    Is it okay for kids competing?

    Many young athletes drink it at tournaments. If you’d rather skip the lion’s mane or L-theanine, you can leave them out when you order.

    Does All Day have caffeine?

    Yes, a little, from the matcha. The other three are caffeine-free.

    Who delivers?

    Our own crew, around Pomona and nearby cities. Your tip goes to the person who brings it.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes, until we start packing your order, for a full refund.
