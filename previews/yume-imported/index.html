About Yume Imported Yume 夢 means “dream” in Japanese.

Yume Imported started out as a hobby, and quickly turn into a direct source to bring you parts, cars and collectibles. Established in 2014 with 15 years experience in all things Japanese we know the business and seen it change throughout the years.

We offer space in container to ship parts, and specialize in off road vehicles that are used for competition or company product display. There are so many amazing things Japan has to offer, Yume can make it happen for you. Browse around or Contact us for any questions. Domo

How it works

  1. Share what you want

    Tell Yume the model, part number, or listing you found. Include any fitment details that matter to your build.

  2. Confirm the details

    Check sourcing options, estimated costs, and available container space before you decide.

    Yume contacts customers once their shipment arrives. Ask the shop about onward shipping if you’re outside California.
