Privacy Policy

Locked In · Last updated: July 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Christian Sandoval ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Locked In mobile application and related services (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide:

  • Account information — your email address, password (stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider), and chosen username.
  • Profile and health/fitness data — height, weight, target weight, age, sex, activity level, and goal; your weigh-in history; food and meal logs (foods, calories, macronutrients, meal times); water intake; logged workouts and activities (type, duration, distance, calories, and GPS route coordinates); interval workouts; and your participation in competitions, friend connections, and reactions.
  • Meal photos — if you use AI photo logging, the image you capture is sent for analysis to generate a calorie and macro estimate.
  • Voice input — if you use voice logging, the audio is sent for transcription to create a food entry.

Information collected automatically:

  • Location — with your permission, precise device location to record GPS workout routes, and approximate location to find nearby restaurants. You can disable location access in your device settings.
  • Device and usage data — basic technical information such as device type, app version, and interactions needed to operate and improve the App.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active trial or paid subscription. We do not receive or store your payment card details (Apple processes payments).

2. How we use your information

  • To provide core features — calorie/macro targets, logging, progress tracking, activities, competitions, and reminders.
  • To generate AI estimates from photos and voice input.
  • To look up nutrition and restaurant information.
  • To operate social features you choose to use (friends, competitions, shared activity).
  • To manage your account, trial, and subscription.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

3. How information is shared

With other users (only what you choose): If you use social features, your username and relevant activity may be visible to others — for example, your standing and score on a competition leaderboard, weight progress within a shared competition, and activities or routes you post to a competition feed, along with reactions. Do not share data you wish to keep private.

With service providers ("subprocessors") who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — backend hosting, authentication, and database storage.
  • OpenAI — processing of meal photos and voice input to generate estimates/transcriptions.
  • FatSecret — nutrition and restaurant database lookups.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and status.
  • Apple — App Store distribution, in-app purchases/payments, and (if enabled) push notifications.
  • Resend — sending transactional emails such as verification and password-reset codes.

Legal and safety: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users or others.

We do not sell your personal information, and the App does not show third-party ads.

4. Data retention

We retain your account and logged data while your account is active. Meal photos and voice recordings are processed to produce your estimate and are not retained for longer than necessary to provide that result, except for the resulting food entry you choose to save.

5. Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account at any time in the App (Profile → Delete account). This removes your account and associated data, including logs, weigh-ins, activities, friends, and competition data. You may also contact us at privacy@gigglez.dev for assistance.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

  • California (CCPA/CPRA): You may request access to or deletion of your personal information and are entitled to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information.
  • EU/UK (GDPR): Our legal bases include performing our contract with you, your consent (e.g., location, photos, voice), and our legitimate interests. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any right, email privacy@gigglez.dev.

9. International users

Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our subprocessors operate, which may have different data protection laws than your country.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date, and where appropriate we will provide additional notice.
